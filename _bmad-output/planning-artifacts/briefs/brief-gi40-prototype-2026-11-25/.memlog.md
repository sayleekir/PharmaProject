topic: GI 4.0 patient leaflet web platform (PharmaProtect Solutions)
updated: 2026-11-25T13:37
---

- (decision) Product is a responsive WEBSITE, not a native app: Figma tech-ecosystem board rejects separate iOS/Android apps as costly.
- (decision) No login, no server-side personal data; recent searches stored only on the user's device and deletable (board: data storage + privacy stories).
- (decision) Accessibility is highest priority: target group is mostly old/sick people and helpers (board). Large tap targets, audio option, no voice-only features.
- (decision) AI limited to speech-to-text, photo-to-text, text-to-speech and suggestion/auto-correct. No AI summarisation or translation (legally not allowed). Leaflets shown verbatim in original language.
- (decision) Only latest approved leaflets (from MAH / pharma association) are shown; version + publish date and a NEW tag displayed.
- (decision) Branding follows PharmaProtect Solutions GmbH (navy/terracotta, Barlow, network hero); product mark is 'GI 4.0 PharmaProtect' in the family style of ACS PharmaProtect.
- (decision) Stakeholder removed the text-size control from the header (kept as deferred accessibility feature; browser zoom works).
- (assumption) Prototype uses mock leaflet data (9 medicines incl. 4 German originals); real data comes from the company database later.
- (assumption) Printing a leaflet is in the story map but is not feasible inside the hosted prototype frame; deferred.
- (assumption) Dummy leaflet data (30 invented medicines, flagged sample) is added for demonstration purposes only; not real medical information.
